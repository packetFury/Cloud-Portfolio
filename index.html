Welcome to packetFury's Arcanum

Hello! My name is packetFury. I am an information security engineer with over five years of traditional SOC engineering experience, and I am currently branching out into the world of DevSecOps.

This website portfolio project is my own personal spin on the Cloud Resume Challenge, which is designed to demonstrate cloud engineering skills. I have gone a bit beyond the original scope of the challenge to implement Infrastructure as Code and CI/CD workflows to emulate a real-world cloud deployment and practice DevSecOps concepts.

For further details on the architecture and design choices for this project, I encourage you to check out the repo.

This is a living project that will continually evolve over my journey into DevSecOps. I hope you enjoy your visit!
